Offering and governance

  • Class A common stock, applied to list on the Nasdaq Global Select Market as ANT; share count and price range blank.
  • Public benefit corporation. Class T stock elects the Class T directors, a board majority (linked to the Long-Term Benefit Trust); Class F stock carries a fixed 50.1% of total voting power; Class E stock goes to employees (excluding co-founders), and a Class N class also exists.
  • Representatives: Goldman Sachs and Morgan Stanley; underwriters have a 30-day over-allotment option.
  • Public offerings outside the US: Australia, Canada, certain EEA states, Japan, Switzerland, UK (no listing there). Singapore: institutional/accredited investors only; no public offering in Taiwan.
  • Staged lockup; see the lockup calendar.
