An IPO does not necessarily trade at 9:30. A Nasdaq IPO opens with a 10-minute Display-Only period (orders accepted, indicative price and imbalance published), followed by a Pre-Launch period in which the lead underwriter/stabilization agent and Nasdaq decide when to run the IPO Cross[src][src]. The exact time of the first trade can't be known in advance.

Who can get an IPO allocation, by region

Two different things: an IPO allocation (buying at the offer price before listing) and trading after listing (at market prices). This section is about the first; the second is generally available anywhere through a broker offering US stocks.

Canada

The S-1 says the offering “includes public offerings in Australia, Canada, certain member states of the European Economic Area, Japan, Switzerland, and the United Kingdom”, without listing there verified[src][src]. Wealthsimple's policy: US IPOs marketed into Canada by prospectus are open to eligible clients, with a 60-day no-flipping policy, a 20% FX buffer, a 2-business-day withdrawal right and allocations confirmed on the morning of closing verified[src][src]. Whether Wealthsimple will offer ANT: unconfirmed.

Singapore

The S-1 says the prospectus is not registered with MAS and the shares may be offered in Singapore only to institutional investors (SFA s.274) and accredited investors (s.275) verified[src][src]. moomoo SG: US IPO subscription is open only to accredited investors, or to non-accredited investors with a minimum subscription of SGD 200,000 verified[src]. Whether moomoo SG gets ANT allocation: unconfirmed.

Hong Kong

We found no Hong Kong-specific selling section in the S-1. Futu Securities (HK) says only Professional Investor (PI) clients can take part in US IPOs; an individual PI needs a portfolio of at least HK$8 million or equivalent verified[src][src]. Rules at other HK brokers (Tiger, uSMART, etc.): unconfirmed.

Taiwan

The S-1 says the offering is not registered with Taiwan's FSC and may not be offered or sold in Taiwan through a public offering; no one in Taiwan is authorized to intermediate the offering verified[src][src]. Whether Taiwanese sub-brokerage (複委託) accounts can participate: unconfirmed.

FAQ

When is the Anthropic IPO?

As of 2026-10-28 Anthropic has filed its S-1 but has not set a price, share count or listing date. News reports say trading could begin as early as November 16, 2026; that is reported, not confirmed by the company.

What is Anthropic's ticker?

The S-1 says Anthropic applied to list its Class A common stock on the Nasdaq Global Select Market under the symbol ANT.

Can I subscribe to the Anthropic IPO in Singapore?

The S-1 says the offering is not registered with the Monetary Authority of Singapore and may be offered in Singapore only to institutional investors (SFA s.274) and accredited investors (s.275). Retail investors generally can only trade after listing.

What time does ANT start trading in Singapore?

After US daylight time ends on November 1, 2026, the regular 9:30 ET Nasdaq open is 22:30 Singapore/Hong Kong time. An IPO's first trade comes from the Nasdaq IPO Cross, timed by the lead underwriter with Nasdaq, so the exact time cannot be known in advance.
