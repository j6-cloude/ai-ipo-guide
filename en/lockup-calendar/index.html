What a lockup is

Pre-IPO holders (founders, employees, early investors) usually agree with the underwriters not to sell for a period after listing. Recent mega-IPOs use staged releases keyed to day counts, earnings dates and sometimes price conditions. An unlock means shares may be sold, not that they will be.

Anthropic (ANT) lockup structure

ANT hasn't priced, so dates can't be computed yet. Structure disclosed in the S-1 verified[src]:

  • Non-founder directors/officers and certain holders: up to 180 days, or earlier on the 2nd full trading day after Q1 2027 earnings.
  • Certain holders and the 7 co-founders: up to 365 days, or earlier after Q3 2027 earnings.
  • Timed partial releases on days 27, 48, 62 and 76 after the prospectus date, and at points after FY2026 and Q1/Q2/Q3 2027 earnings; percentages are blank in the S-1.
  • Morgan Stanley and Goldman Sachs may waive the restrictions.

Once ANT prices, enter the prospectus date above to get days 27/48/62/76/180/365.
